**Figure 1. Gene-expression differences and GO Biological Process enrichment in IgAN versus controls.**
**(A)** Volcano plot of 18,756 genes from 43 IgAN and 22 control samples.
The x-axis shows apeglm-shrunken log2 fold changes; the y-axis shows
−log10(BH-adjusted Wald-test p-values). Orange and blue indicate significantly
higher and lower expression in IgAN, respectively (adjusted p < 0.05); grey
indicates nonsignificant genes. The horizontal dashed line marks the significance
cutoff. Vertical lines mark twofold differences and were not used as significance
thresholds. Labels identify the five named significant genes with the smallest
raw p-values in each direction.
**(B)** Eight representative enriched GO BP terms per direction, selected after
reducing redundancy using Wang semantic similarity (cutoff 0.7), favouring terms
with smaller adjusted p-values. Enrichment used an effective background of
13,719 GO-annotated genes, with BH correction applied separately within each
direction. Horizontal position shows the percentage of GO BP-annotated input
genes contributing to each term; point area shows gene count and colour shows
−log10(BH-adjusted enrichment p-value). The 37 contributors to “negative regulation
of myoblast fusion” were ribosomal genes with NAS evidence; this result does not
establish increased myoblast fusion in kidney tissue.
